Good to Know

Do you deliver outside India?

Yes. Enter your country in the order form and we will confirm shipping charges and delivery time on WhatsApp.

How does Cash on Delivery work?

Within India, you pay on delivery. A COD charge of ₹50 is added to the order.

How should I store the gud?

Keep it in a clean, dry, airtight container, away from moisture. Dry-fruit varieties are best in a cool place.
